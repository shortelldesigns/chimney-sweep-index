Have the chimney checked before the first fire

An independent homeowner index for chimney sweeping, creosote, and dryer vents. It points you to official finders and state contractor lookups. It does not invent sweep names, and it does not scrape CSIA’s roster for marketing.

How to find a sweep. CSIA publishes a public finder for homeowners at web.csia.org/directory. CSIA states that list is a public service for locating a professional or verifying credentials, and is not intended for marketing or business development. Use CSIA’s own site. Then check any required state or local contractor registration on the official lookup for that state.

How-to

When to sweep a chimney

NFPA 211 calls for an annual inspection. Sweeping is a separate step, when deposits warrant it. Fall is the practical window before heating season.

Chimney fires

Creosote and chimney fires

What creosote is, why it burns, signs a chimney fire may have already happened, and what CSIA and fire-service sources say to do next.

Not a chimney

Dryer vents are not chimneys

Lint is a different fuel, on a different path. A clothes dryer should not exhaust into a chimney. CSIA’s CDET credential is the dryer-exhaust counterpart.

State pages (verify on official lookups)

These pages do not list contractor names. No public statewide chimney-sweep roster suitable for republication was found. Honest verify pages beat invented names.

Pennsylvania

VerifyCSIA finder + PA HIC search

No statewide sweep license. Home-improvement contractors over the HICPA threshold register with the Attorney General. Chimney Sweep is a Type of Work in that search.

Massachusetts

VerifyCSIA finder + MA HIC hub

Home Improvement Contractor registration for work on existing 1–4 unit owner-occupied homes. Separate from a Construction Supervisor License.

Maryland

VerifyCSIA finder + MHIC query

Maryland Labor does not license chimney cleaning. Caps, rebuilds, and other home-improvement chimney work need an MHIC contractor license.

Virginia

VerifyCSIA finder + DPOR lookup

DPOR Board for Contractors licenses firms by class and specialty. There is no chimney-sweep-only statewide roster.
